How it plays

Each of the seven sectors has an inland point, a coast point and a sea point. They are taken in order along each line, and a headquarters can only be attacked once every other point on the map has fallen.

Land points are nudged onto dry land and sea points out to sea when the battle starts. Mountains and hills are flattened around points, headquarters and airfields (the dashed rings).

If water splits the coast line, the map plays as an island campaign: ground forces land by sea.

Mountains and hills slow vehicles and block shells. Forests slow ground forces but hide them and absorb damage. Water carves lakes, rivers and channels. Flatten erases terrain.
